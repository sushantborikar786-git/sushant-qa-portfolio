01 Analyze requirements and acceptance criteria thoroughly to identify test scenarios before development begins
02 Challenge assumptions by asking clarifying questions and identifying gaps in requirements early
03 Design test cases that cover positive paths, negative scenarios, edge cases, and boundary conditions
04 Automate repetitive test scenarios and regression cycles to enable continuous validation and faster feedback
05 Document and communicate defects with clarity, reproducibility, and severity assessment for efficient resolution
